UNDERSTANDING THE TECHNOLOGY

What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence enables computers and machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning and decision-making.

01

Learn

AI systems can learn patterns from data and improve their performance over time.

02

Understand

Modern AI can process language, images, sounds and complex information.

03

Create

Generative AI can produce text, images, code, audio and other digital content.

1980s

Expert Systems

2010s

Deep Learning
